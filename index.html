bio

My academic background is in philosophy, having trained in various universities in the U.S., France, and Portugal. My research focuses on the nature of normativity, especially as it arises in logic and ethics. I am currently developing the idea that concepts and actions get their content from the same rules, namely, those that allow us to unify ourselves. Alongside my philosophical interests, I am a practicing guitarist and composer with a specific focus on improvised musics, especially jazz. I am currently based in Kobe, Japan, where I teach mathematics at a secondary school, engage in Japanese-English translation work, and run the book division of Okuba.
